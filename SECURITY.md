# Security Policy

Thank you for helping improve Mondi Radio devices.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately, not as a public issue on this repository.

- **Email:** <will@imbas.tech>
- **Subject:** `SECURITY:` followed by a short description.

Include, if you can:

- A clear description of the issue and its potential impact.
- Firmware version(s) affected. The running version is shown on the device's status screen.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
- Whether you have already disclosed this to anyone else.

If you would prefer to encrypt correspondence, ask for a PGP key in your first email and one will be provided for follow-up.

## What to expect

- **Acknowledgement:** within 3 business days.
- **Initial triage and severity assessment:** within 10 business days.
- **Fix and coordinated disclosure:** depends on severity, typically 30–90 days from initial acknowledgement. We will keep you informed while we work on a fix.

You will be credited in the release notes for the fix, under your preferred name or anonymously, whichever you choose.

## Scope

The Mondi Radio relies on numerous third-party services, especially publicly-streaming FM radios, certain API providers, Github, internet service providers, etc. Any issues discovered in such services are outside the domain of this project, please do not report any such failures.

If, however, you find bugs or issues with the firmware, device behavior, cryptographic verification, OTA updates, etc., or any other issues originating with the device itself, please submit a report.
